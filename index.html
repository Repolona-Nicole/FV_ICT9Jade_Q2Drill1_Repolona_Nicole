⚽ Beginner's Guide to Soccer

📋 Basic Rules

  • No hands or arms (except goalie)
  • 90-minute match (two halves)
  • Offside rule
  • Throw-ins, corner goal kicks

🥅 Soccer Equipment

  • Soccer ball
  • Cleats (football boots)
  • Shin guards long socks
  • Goalposts pitch

🎯 Basic Techniques

  • Dribbling and ball control
  • Short and long passing
  • Shooting and finishing
  • Defensive marking tackling
